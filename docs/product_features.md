# Product Details & Categories (Easy Guide)

Welcome! This document explains how products work on the ExportHub platform in simple, everyday language. 

Because we sell very different types of products—like **Clothing (Apparel)** and **Packaged Food**—the platform is smart enough to change how it looks and behaves depending on what you are looking at.

---

## 1. Two Types of Products

When a product is added to the system, it is categorized. The two main types you will see are:
1. **Standard/Apparel:** Items like t-shirts, jackets, or accessories.
2. **Packaged Food:** Items like coffee, snacks, or bottled sauces.

The system automatically knows that a t-shirt needs a "Size" and "Color", but a bag of coffee needs a "Weight" and an "Expiration Date". 

---

## 2. What the Customer Sees (The Storefront)

When a customer clicks on a product to buy it, the page will magically shape-shift to fit the item!

### If they are buying Clothing:
* **Options:** They will see standard buttons to choose their **Size** (e.g., Small, Medium) and **Color** (e.g., Red, Blue).
* **Information Tabs:** At the bottom, they can read the item's Description, what Materials it is made of, and a Size Guide.

### If they are buying Packaged Food:
* **Options:** Instead of size and color, they will see buttons for **Flavor** (e.g., Spicy, Mild) and **Weight/Volume** (e.g., 250g, 500g).
* **Safety Warnings:** Right above the "Add to Cart" button, there is a highly visible warning box that lists any **Allergens** (like Peanuts or Dairy) so the customer cannot miss it.
* **Dietary Badges:** Under the price, there are green badges highlighting dietary features, like "Vegan" or "Gluten-Free".
* **Information Tabs:** At the bottom, they can read the Description, the full **Ingredients List**, the **Nutritional Facts** (like calories), and **Storage Instructions** (like "Keep Refrigerated").

---

## 3. What the Staff Sees (The Admin Panel)

When a staff member or manager is adding or editing a product in the Admin Dashboard, the form is also smart.

* **General Info:** You fill out the name, price, and description for everything.
* **Smart Variants:** If you tell the system the product is "Food", it stops asking you for shirt sizes. Instead, it asks you for the Weight, Flavor, and Pack Size.
* **The Compliance Tab:** If the product is Food, a special "Compliance" tab appears. This is a mandatory section where staff must type in the Ingredients, Allergens, Dietary Tags, and Shelf Life. 

### A Note on Allergens and Tags
When typing in Allergens or Dietary Tags, you can just type them normally with commas (e.g., `Peanuts, Dairy, Soy`). The system will automatically separate them into neat little tags when you click Save.

---

## 4. Behind the Scenes (Logistics & Inventory)

We also track inventory differently based on the product.
* **Batch Tracking:** For food, we don't just track that we have "50 bags of coffee." We track that we have "30 bags from Batch A (expiring in December)" and "20 bags from Batch B (expiring in January)".
* **Regional Warehouses:** The system strictly checks if an item is physically in the customer's country. If the UK warehouse runs out of coffee, UK customers will see it as "Out of Stock," even if the Nepal warehouse has plenty.

This smart division ensures that ExportHub can sell anything, while keeping customers safe and logistics accurate!
